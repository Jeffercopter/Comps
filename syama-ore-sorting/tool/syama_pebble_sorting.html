Basis and limitations

Syama values retained: ROM grade, gold recovery, gold price (Resolute disclosure) and the measured scat grade (client sampling). Ratios and unit rates where Syama site data was unavailable are carried from a 2024 African gold mine pebble sorting study: dilution, scat load, sorter duty, costs, operating hours, discount rate and evaluation life. Dilution is the carry-over most worth challenging — a sublevel cave typically runs higher than the reference, and dilution drives the back-solved contrast. Sorter duty requires test work; a mass contrast is not evidence of a detectable sensor contrast. This tool supports a decision to fund test work, not a decision to buy plant.
