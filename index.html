Both models are evaluated on the mill the DEM is running — same D, L, % critical, Jc, Jb and densities — as an independent reference for the torque-derived number. Cylinder only: no cone ends, since the simulated cross-section has none. Morrell tracks the DEM to within a few per cent across the range. Austin, with the constant as supplied, sits about 1.7× Morrell — treat it as unc­alibrated rather than as a second opinion. On overflow, Morrell applies a fixed-toe correction and drops sharply; the DEM has no slurry pool and cannot reproduce that, so compare on grate.
